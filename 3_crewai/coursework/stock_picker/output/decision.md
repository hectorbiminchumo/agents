Nvidia is the best balance of:
- **Growth**
- **Strategic dominance**
- **Ecosystem strength**
- **Future optionality**

## Companies not selected

### Broadcom
Broadcom is a very strong company and an excellent AI infrastructure compounder, but it was not selected because:
- It is **less direct and less dominant** than Nvidia in the AI compute stack
- Its upside is likely **more durable but less explosive**
- Much of its appeal comes from **networking, enterprise software, and VMware integration**, which are attractive but not as compelling as Nvidia’s core AI leadership

Broadcom remains a high-quality candidate, especially for investors seeking a more diversified, cash-generative infrastructure and software business. However, it does not match Nvidia’s combination of dominance and growth momentum.

### MediaTek
MediaTek is improving and may benefit from the Nvidia partnership, but it was not selected because:
- It is still more exposed to **smartphone and consumer device cycles**
- Its AI PC opportunity is promising but **not yet proven at the same scale**
- It lacks Nvidia’s same level of **moat, brand strength, and ecosystem control**

MediaTek could become a stronger investment if the AI PC market develops quickly and the Nvidia partnership converts into meaningful design wins. For now, it is more of a secondary beneficiary than the primary leader.

### OpenAI
OpenAI is one of the most influential AI companies in the world, but it was not selected because:
- It is **not directly investable in the public market**
- It faces **governance, regulatory, and capital-intensity risks**
- Its commercial relationships and distribution strategy can be complex and constrained

From a technology leadership perspective, OpenAI is exceptional. But as an investment choice, it is less practical because of limited direct public-market access.

### Enflame
Enflame was not selected because:
- It is a **higher-risk China-specific semiconductor play**
- It faces major **geopolitical and export-control risks**
- It likely trails leading global players in technology depth and market reach
- IPO and liquidity dynamics add additional uncertainty

Enflame could offer meaningful upside in a domestic China AI chip scenario, but it is significantly riskier and less proven than Nvidia.
